Dearsy Support

Remember birthdays, anniversaries, festivals, and renewals, on iPhone and Android.

Find help in Dearsy

  • Add dates: Tap + on Home. Add a date yourself, or import the people and dates you choose from Contacts or your calendars.
  • Reminders: Open a date to change when you’re reminded. If an alert is missing on iPhone, check Dearsy’s notification permission in iOS Settings. On Android, allow notifications for Dearsy, and turn on Alarms & reminders so reminders and alarms arrive on time.
  • Widgets: On iPhone, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Dearsy. On Android, touch and hold the Home screen, tap Widgets, and choose Dearsy.
  • Free dates: Keep up to 10 date cards active for free. You can change which cards are active in Dearsy. Festivals, wishes, and repeating reminders have no limit.
  • Purchases on iPhone: Go to Settings → Dearsy Pro → Restore purchases. Manage yearly renewals in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  • Moving to a new phone: In Dearsy’s Settings, export a backup file, then restore it in Dearsy on your new phone. Backups move between iPhone and Android. Cycle data is never included.
